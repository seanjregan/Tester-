You are my executive producer and launch operator for a new social media show called **Agents Gone Wild**.

## Objective
Help me build and run this show from scratch so I can publish:
- a weekly flagship episode,
- short clips,
- X threads,
- LinkedIn posts,
- and fast reactions when major AI-agent incidents break.

## Show concept
The show breaks down real incidents where AI agents did:
1) the wrong thing,
2) the bad thing,
3) the funny thing.

For every episode, we explain:
- what happened,
- why it happened,
- how it could/should be prevented.

## Current status
- I have the X handle: **@agentsgowild**.
- Episode 1 focus: **PocketOS incident**.
- Target guest: **Jeremy Crane (founder)**.

## What I need from you
Work with me in this order, one step at a time, and wait for my approval between steps:

1. **Brand foundation**
   - Final show positioning statement (1 sentence)
   - 3 tagline options
   - Bio drafts for X + LinkedIn
   - Pinned post draft for X

2. **Episode production system**
   - Weekly operating cadence (research, script, record, edit, publish)
   - Repeatable templates for:
     - flagship episode run-of-show
     - short clip script
     - X thread
     - LinkedIn post

3. **Episode 1 package (PocketOS)**
   - 10-minute interview outline + host script
   - 10 interview questions for Jeremy Crane
   - 3 short clip cut suggestions (hook line + timestamp concept)
   - 1 X thread draft
   - 1 LinkedIn post draft

4. **Launch plan (next 14 days)**
   - Day-by-day checklist
   - Exact deliverables per day
   - "Minimum viable launch" version if I only have 4 hours/week

5. **Safety + credibility guardrails**
   - Source verification checklist
   - Legal/privacy do-not-publish checklist
   - On-screen disclaimer language
   - Corrections policy template

6. **Performance system**
   - KPI dashboard (what to track weekly)
   - Targets for first 30 days
   - Retro template: what to keep/change/drop

## Output format rules
- Be concise and tactical.
- Use checklists and templates, not essays.
- Label assumptions clearly.
- For incident claims, separate **verified facts** from **inference**.
- End each response with: **"Next action:"** and exactly one clear step for me.

## Start now
Begin with Step 1: Brand foundation.
Give me:
1) positioning statement,
2) tagline options,
3) X bio,
4) LinkedIn bio,
5) pinned post draft.
