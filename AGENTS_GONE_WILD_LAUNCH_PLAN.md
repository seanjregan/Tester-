# Agents Gone Wild — Build Plan

## 1) Show Positioning

**Core promise:** Break down real AI-agent failures in a way that's useful, entertaining, and practical.

**Audience:**
- Builders (PMs, engineers, founders)
- Operators (support, security, risk teams)
- Curious professionals who are AI-adjacent

**Episode formula (repeatable):**
1. **The incident** (what happened)
2. **The root cause** (why it happened)
3. **The prevention** (what to do differently)
4. **The takeaway** (one-line lesson)

Tagline idea: **"When agents go off script, we go under the hood."**

---

## 2) Content Pillars

Use these to keep episodes varied but coherent:

1. **Wrong Thing** — harm, policy breach, data leak, unauthorized action.
2. **Bad Thing** — business/legal/reputation damage from poor guardrails.
3. **Funny Thing** — harmless chaos, weird loops, prompt hijinks.
4. **Near Misses** — incidents that were caught early (great for prevention lessons).

Create a weekly mix (example):
- 1 major breakdown episode
- 2 short clips from episode
- 1 carousel/text post with prevention checklist
- 1 “news break” quick reaction when relevant

---

## 3) Episode Formats for X and LinkedIn

### A) Flagship Weekly Episode (6–12 minutes)
- Host on video platform (YouTube or native uploads).
- Publish teaser + thread on X.
- Publish native video + text summary on LinkedIn.

### B) Shorts (30–90 seconds)
- "Incident in 60 seconds"
- "One mistake, one fix"

### C) Thread/Carousel Breakdown
- Slide/Post 1: Headline incident
- Slide/Post 2: What failed
- Slide/Post 3: Why safeguards failed
- Slide/Post 4: Prevention checklist
- Slide/Post 5: Your opinion / CTA

---

## 4) Weekly Production Workflow (from scratch)

### Monday — Research & Selection
- Monitor incidents from security blogs, model provider updates, AI/dev communities, X, LinkedIn.
- Score candidate incidents with this rubric (1–5 each):
  - Impact
  - Novelty
  - Evidence quality
  - Teachability
  - Virality potential

### Tuesday — Script
- Build script in this structure:
  - Hook (15 sec)
  - Story timeline (90 sec)
  - Root-cause analysis (2–4 min)
  - Prevention framework (2–4 min)
  - CTA (15 sec)

### Wednesday — Record
- Batch record flagship + 2 short clips.
- Capture clean A-roll plus 3–5 B-roll/screenshots.

### Thursday — Edit and Package
- Edit long episode + shorts.
- Prepare captions/subtitles.
- Create thumbnail and title variants.

### Friday — Publish & Engage
- Publish on X + LinkedIn.
- Respond to comments in first 60–90 minutes.
- Collect analytics after 24 hours.

---

## 5) Brand and Voice Guidelines

**Voice:**
- Sharp, non-hysterical, evidence-based, slightly playful.
- Avoid fearmongering.

**Principles:**
- Critique systems, not individuals.
- Separate verified facts from inference.
- Always include prevention guidance.

**Visual identity starter:**
- Dark background + warning accent color.
- Repeating frame: "Incident → Cause → Prevention".
- Consistent lower-third and title card.

## 9) Risk, Legal, and Ethics Guardrails

Before publishing an incident:
- Verify with at least two credible sources where possible.
- Redact private/sensitive data.
- Avoid naming individuals unless already public and necessary.
- Label speculation clearly.
- Include correction policy in profile/bio or pinned post.

Use this disclaimer pattern:
- "This analysis is based on publicly available information as of [date]."

## 10) Templates You Can Reuse

### A) Episode Script Template
- **Hook:** "This AI agent did X. Everyone blamed Y. The real problem was Z."
- **Context:** Who/what/when.
- **Failure chain:** Step-by-step.
- **Root cause:** Technical and process causes.
- **Prevention:** 3 controls (design, policy, monitoring).
- **Closing:** One-sentence operator takeaway.

### B) X Thread Template
1. Hook + stakes
2. What happened
3. Why it mattered
4. Technical failure point
5. Process failure point
6. The fix that would've prevented this
7. Checklist
8. CTA question

### C) LinkedIn Post Template
- Hook line
- Short incident summary
- Why systems failed (3 bullets)
- Prevention checklist (3 bullets)
- Ask for perspective from practitioners

## 12) Metrics That Matter Early

Primary:
- 3-second and 30-second video retention
- Saves/bookmarks/shares
- Comments per 1,000 impressions
- Followers from non-follower reach

Secondary:
- Link clicks
- Profile visits
- Newsletter signups (if you add one)

Set a weekly review ritual:
- What hook worked?
- Which segment dropped viewers?
- Which prevention tips drove comments?
